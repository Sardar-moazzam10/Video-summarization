# Blind Human Pairwise Evaluation Protocol

## Objective
Evaluate the narrative flow, ordering, and structural coherence of multi-video summaries produced by two different methods (e.g., Full VidFusion vs Full-minus-KnowledgePlan).

## Methodology
- **Blind**: Reviewers must not know which method produced Summary A or Summary B.
- **Pairwise**: Reviewers watch/read both summaries generated from the exact same input videos and duration target, then compare them.

## Rating Template
For each pair of summaries (A and B), answer the following on a scale of -2 to +2:
*(-2: A is significantly better, 0: Equal/Unsure, +2: B is significantly better)*

1. **Logical Flow & Topic Progression**
   *Does the summary move logically from one topic to the next, building up concepts appropriately, or does it feel scattered?*
   Score (-2 to +2): ___

2. **Explanation Continuity**
   *Do consecutive segments make sense together? Are there jarring jumps mid-explanation?*
   Score (-2 to +2): ___

3. **Source Transitions**
   *When the summary switches from one video source to another, does the transition feel natural or abrupt?*
   Score (-2 to +2): ___

## Overall Preference
Taking all factors into account, which summary provides a better viewing/learning experience?
- [ ] Summary A
- [ ] Summary B
- [ ] No preference / Both equal

*(Note: Do not report fabricated results. This template is ready for actual human testers.)*
